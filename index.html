About

I'm a software engineer at Vmax Concept Group. I build the systems that restaurants and small businesses run on every day: a POS with cashier, customer and QR ordering apps, a middleware that sends e-invoices to LHDN, and an NFC business card app.

I didn't start in software. My degree at UiTM was in computer networking, and my internship was at the Terengganu state data centre, keeping servers, switches and routers running for government offices. That background still shapes how I work. I think about where an app will be deployed, what the network looks like, and what happens when something goes down.

At Vmax I work across the whole cycle: gathering requirements, building features in Flutter and Laravel, testing, deploying to cPanel and writing the documentation. A lot of it is integration work, like payment gateways, the LHDN API, NFC and OCR, and making separate systems talk to each other reliably.

Outside of work I've built a SIEM dashboard on Elastic Stack, an ESP32-CAM surveillance system, and an indoor navigation app for Pasar Payang as my final year project.
